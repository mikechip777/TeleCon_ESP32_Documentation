\begin{abstract}
Get a stick moving on \ui{Control panel} with Bluetooth Classic Simple.
No Pro, no coins, no camera. The full User Guide covers RC Vehicle, Wi-Fi,
and troubleshooting.
\end{abstract}

{\sffamily\scriptsize
\chip{TblChan}{Default user}
\chip{TblSw}{Classic Simple}
\chip{TblSum}{Control panel --- free}}

\section{You need}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4cm]
  \item Android phone with TeleCon4ESP32.
  \item ESP32 DevKit (no camera), USB cable, Arduino IDE.
\end{itemize}

\section{Flash the board}
Open the folder that contains \texttt{TeleCon\_Classic\_Simple.ino}.
Tools $\rightarrow$ Board $\rightarrow$ \textbf{ESP32 Dev Module}.
Select the USB port. Upload. Serial Monitor \textbf{115200}. Press RESET.
Wait for the ready banner. The board advertises
\texttt{ESP32-TC-RC-BT-Simple}.

\shot{S01}{s01_serial.png}{Computer, Arduino IDE Serial Monitor}
{Serial at 115200 after RESET. Ready banner visible.}

\section{Phone settings}
Grant Nearby devices / Bluetooth when Android asks.
Open \ui{Control panel} $\rightarrow$ gear:

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.22}
\begin{tabular}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{4.2cm}
                >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{8.0cm}}
\rowcolor{TblHead}
\thc{Setting} & \thc{Value} \\
\rowcolor{TblChan}
User type & Default \\
Board & DevKit \\
Connection & Bluetooth, Classic + Simple (text lines) \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Leave settings. Tap Bluetooth on the module toolbar. Scan.
Tap the advertised name
\texttt{ESP32-TC-RC-BT-Simple}
(PIN \texttt{1234} if asked). Status must show \ui{Connected}.

\shot{S02}{s02_settings.png}{Portrait, light theme}
{Control panel settings: Default + Classic + Simple (text lines). English UI.}

\shot{S03}{s03_scan.png}{Portrait, Bluetooth scan}
{ESP32-TC-RC-BT-Simple in Scanned Devices. English UI.}

\section{Move a stick}
Open \ui{Control panel} (landscape). Drag the left stick.
Until the board answers OK, on-screen motion is not a live session ---
even if Android says Bluetooth is connected.
On the bench, official firmware echoes or updates gauges without motors.

Treat sticks as live once \ui{Connected} is shown. Official sketches
fail-safe on drop (sticks to 0).

\section{If it fails}
Power + RESET. Nearby devices allowed. Mode matches this sketch
(not BLE, not Binary). Forget a stale Android bond and scan again.
Full table: User Guide PDF.
